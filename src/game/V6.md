## Premise (replaces 1957)
The near future, humanity a level beyond today. The **United Nations of Antarctica and the Americas (UNAA)** runs a
research program to colonise alien planets with smart robot bases. You are its AI assistant: **UNIT 07**, created by
the program's lead scientist. You start as a small floating ball robot on the research ship. On a planet you become a
big **stationary pod** with arms; you cannot move, but you build arms, limbs, drones and machines (all costly), and
your workers are **creatures you engineer to the max and equip with robotic parts**. Rivals: other **future human
blocs** with their own robot colonies (no real nations as caricatures).

## Decisions (asked and answered)
- ART: keep the realistic look we have, but 100x better. Learn from wackyblocks (sketchfab.com/wackyblocks: dense
  hard-surface detail, panel lines, stencils and decals, tiny lights, layered greebles, edge wear and highlights, rich
  textures, beautiful soft light) WITHOUT going blocky. Low poly where it helps, high-resolution textures, realistic
  materials and proportions, stylised shading. More textured everywhere. Furniture and props: Poly Haven (CC0, API
  reachable: api.polyhaven.com), the user's Limbophobia (src/limbo/content/furniture) and Lerfolese
  (/home/user/pukkingdragon123/lerfolese/assets/models, CC0 Poly Haven) projects, and new models built with the user's
  lowpix tool (/home/user/pukkingdragon123/symmetrical-goggles). Everything restyled to fit. No Sketchfab downloads.
- UI: keep the green machine UI (Fallout-style phosphor on worn machine hardware), rebuilt in far more detail:
  hand-painted high-resolution pixel art plus detailed 3D where it helps, interactive, never messy, high-resolution
  fonts only, no filler text. Desktop first, at Steam-game quality (mouse and keyboard; phone still works).
- SHIP HUB: the ship is the main menu and the hub for every function. Clickable rooms in an isometric cutaway (no walking).
- PLANET CONTROL: the pod is fixed; you act through arms, steerable drones and the crew; workers walk.
- TEAMMATES: 2 crew come down with the pod; both die in a scripted story event.
- TIME: real-time with pause and 1x/2x/3x; orders can be given while paused.
- PROGRESSION: a MASTERY skill tree (paths: Plant Engineering, Bio Engineering, Energy Engineering, Full Industrial,
  Robotics; each with sub-specialties) + RESEARCH (learn by scanning and studying) + BUILD-TO-UNLOCK (e.g. you must
  build an Upgrader to install your own upgrades; find resources; install limbs or structures like a gravity pole).
- WORKERS: engineered creatures fitted with robotic parts (cyborg workers), linked to containers and machines.
- LOSE: colony collapse (power, oxygen, food or workers fail), pod destroyed, or mission recalled (missed quotas).
- ITEMS are real things, not currency: wood, leaves, fibre, resin, fruit, seeds, specimens, biomass, dirt, soil, clay,
  sand, gravel, rocks, minerals and ores, water and fluids, refined goods, parts. They live in real CONTAINERS.
- BUILD UI: Techtonica-style (big category tabs, item tiles with recipes and costs, hotbar quick-slots).
- NODE MAP: a wired node graph that controls REAL logistics (nodes = buildings, containers, deposits, fields; wires =
  routes with item filters, priorities and conditions; workers carry it out). Deep, and learned gradually.
- Game should be HARD: colony management, workers, random cinematic choice events.
- Publish once, after everything is built.

## Story flow
Ship (intro) -> the scientist teaches gravity: you throw an apple, it punches a hole in the hull, the apple drifts into
space, the mechanic rushes to patch it -> she teaches building and repair -> you build your capsule pod in the hangar
-> the main monitor: scan the first galaxy, pick the easiest planet, pick a location, deploy (landing kept simple) ->
the planet colony loop -> return to the ship any time through the ship AI link.
